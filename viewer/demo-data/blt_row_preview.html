Vineyard row, real photos

One vine row, both faces, built from 220 photos and depth taken every 0.5 m by a camera on a ground robot driving the alleys. June 22, 2022, Ktima Gerovassiliou, Greece.

Holes are places where the camera saw through the canopy. Only the band the side camera could see (about 0.5 to 1.4 m up) is shown. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

Data: Bacchus Long-Term dataset, University of Lincoln (Polvara et al. 2024), CC BY 4.0.
